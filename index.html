Privacy Policy

App Name: Chair Workout for Women 40+
Package ID: com.amrit.chairworkoutforwomen40

Welcome to Chair Workout for Women 40+ ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (com.amrit.chairworkoutforwomen40) handles, stores, and protects information when you use our app on Android devices.

🔒 Quick Privacy Summary:
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign up, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.
  • 100% On-Device Health & Fitness Data: Your workout history, weight logs, BMI calculations, daily water intake, and fitness preferences are stored exclusively locally on your device. We never upload, sell, or share your personal health data to any external server.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a personalized fitness and hydration experience, the app allows you to enter and track wellness information, including:

  • Onboarding preferences (fitness experience level, focus areas, workout duration, and age bracket).
  • Workout history, completed days in the 28-day plan, active streaks, and estimated calories burned.
  • Body weight logs, target weight goals, height, and BMI calculations.
  • Daily water intake logs (ml / fl oz), hydration goals, and 7-day hydration history.
  • Daily workout and hydration reminder times.

How This Data Is Handled & Android System Backup: All of the wellness data listed above is stored privately on your phone using Android Jetpack DataStore and is never transmitted to our servers. Because Android Auto Backup (android:allowBackup="true") is enabled to help you keep your progress if you switch phones, your Android operating system may include local app preferences in your personal Google account device backup if you have system backups turned on in your phone settings. We never have access to your personal Google backup.

2. Automatically Collected Information (Third-Party SDKs)

While we do not collect personal health data off-device, our app integrates trusted Google services to display advertisements and improve app stability and user experience:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banners and interstitial advertisements that keep our app free. AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (Google Play Services AD_ID).
  • Coarse location data (derived from IP address), device model, operating system version, and language settings.
  • Ad interaction metrics (such as ad impressions and clicks) for fraud prevention and ad delivery.

You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

B. Google Firebase Analytics (Anonymous Usage Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics strictly to understand anonymous app engagement (such as which screens are visited, which workouts are started or completed, and app stability diagnostics). Our analytics implementation strictly filters out and prohibits the transmission of any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), weight numbers, BMI values, or private health records.

Learn more about how Google handles data at: Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Device Permissions We Use

Chair Workout for Women 40+ requests only the minimum Android permissions required for core functionality:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send optional daily workout reminders and gentle daytime water hydration check-ins. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or Android Settings.
  • Scheduled Alarms & Boot Completed (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used strictly to trigger your chosen workout and hydration reminders on time and restore those schedules when your phone restarts.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely to load Google AdMob advertisements and send anonymous Firebase Analytics events.
  • Vibration & Wake Lock (VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK): Used to keep the screen awake during active workout timers and provide gentle feedback cues.

4. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Retention: Local fitness, weight, and hydration data remains on your device for as long as the app is installed or until you delete it. Anonymous analytics and advertising diagnostic data collected by Google Firebase Analytics and AdMob are retained in accordance with Google's standard retention policies (typically 2 to 14 months for analytics events).

Because we do not create user accounts or store your personal fitness data on developer servers, you have direct control over deleting your data:

  • In-App Reset & Deletion: You can reset your workout progress, completed days, streaks, and water logs at any time inside the app via Progress > Settings > More Settings > Reset Progress, and delete individual weight log entries directly in the Weight Tracker history list.
  • Complete Wipe (Device Storage Clear / Uninstall): Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Chair Workout for Women 40+ > Storage > Clear data) or uninstalling the app permanently deletes 100% of your local data, weight logs, and preferences.
  • Advertising ID Deletion: You can delete or reset your Google Advertising ID at any time via your Android device under Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.

5. Health & Medical Disclaimer

Chair Workout for Women 40+ provides low-impact seated exercise routines and hydration tracking for general fitness and educational purposes only. The app is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your physician or qualified healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have pre-existing joint, cardiovascular, or mobility conditions.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed specifically for adults (women aged 40 and older) and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 18). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California), you have the right to know what data is collected, opt out of personalized advertising, and request data deletion. Because all personal wellness data stays locally on your device, you can exercise full deletion rights directly on your device at any time.
